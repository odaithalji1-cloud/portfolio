01. About me

I'm a backend and AI engineer with a B.Sc. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. I like the part of software where data, APIs and models meet — designing clean services, wiring them into real workflows, and making AI features actually useful in production.

Today I work as an integrations developer on an open-source AI workflow-automation platform, building and maintaining pieces, output schemas and AI actions. I'm also the co-founder of Servo, an agentic AI assistant for businesses.

Along the way I've shipped backends for e-learning, commerce, healthcare, safety and industrial IoT platforms — and I keep a few open-source starter kits around so the next project starts faster.
